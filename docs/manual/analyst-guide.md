# SentinelX Security Analyst Playbook

This manual outlines operational guidelines for analysts performing real-time threat investigation and incident containment inside the SentinelX platform.

---

## 1. Spotting Anomalies
1. **Activity Register**: Review the bottom console log. Watch for alerts containing `Suricata` or `Falco` headers.
2. **State Inspection**: Click any warning (orange) or compromised (red) node on the graph map.
3. **Scan Data**: Read the active scan card to review parent-child nodes, software patch version indices, and credential clearances.

---

## 2. Initiating Containment (Manual Quarantine)
If a critical system like `app-payroll` or `payroll-db` shows breach indicators, take immediate corrective actions to limit blast damage:
1. **Select Component**: Click on the compromised host on the map.
2. **De-escalate / Isolate**: Under the node action drawer, click **INITIATE NEURAL ISOLATION**.
3. **Verify Separation**: The node color will transition to deep blue. All links connecting to other network servers will sever to shield your directories.

---

## 3. Investigating with AI assistance
1. Navigate to **AI Reasoning Studio** tab.
2. Here, you will find dynamic Gemini-compiled summaries explaining precisely how the breach occurred and what lines of business are at risk.
3. Follow the **Procedural Recommendations** generated by the AI to resolve vulnerabilities on other adjacent systems before taking the quarantine offline.
