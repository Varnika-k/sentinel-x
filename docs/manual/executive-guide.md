# SentinelX Executive Uptime & Risk Manual

This manual is tailored for Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs), risk directors, and managers seeking to track global uptime, financial risks, and corporate SLA compliance.

---

## 1. Navigating Executive Dashboards
Executives use SentinelX to translate low-level IT logs into strategic board-ready reports:
1. **Enter Command Center**: Choose **Enterprise Command Center** or the **Enterprise OS** tab.
2. **Review Health Score**: On top, the **Global Health Index (0-100)** displays the integrated strength of secure operations.
3. **Audit High-Altitude Metrics**: Monitor Operational, Compliance-Governance, Infrastructure Utilization, and Workforce Trust indices.

---

## 2. Tracking Financial Impact & Downtime Costs
1. **Active Incidents**: Look at the active outage banners on the **Enterprise OS**.
2. **Monetary Loss Calculations**: The system multiplies affected lines of business by pre-configured business-impact values to show a real-time **Financial Impact Score (in USD/hour)**.
3. **Mitigation Cost-Benefit Evaluation**: Decide whether to coordinate immediate system isolation or allow degraded operations to satisfy customer SLA guidelines.

---

## 3. Formulating Root-Cause AI Audits
1. Open the **Enterprise Memory Registers**.
2. Review historic incident folders to identify persistent regression vectors (e.g., repeating credential leakage or third-party API compromises).
3. Print or download the exportable audit logs for statutory compliance records.
